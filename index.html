About Mistora

An Estate Retreat, Made for Slowing Down

Tucked away amid the coffee estates and misty hills of Coorg, Mistora Estate Villa is a private retreat designed for travellers who want more than just a place to sleep. This is a home away from home — spacious, comfortable, and surrounded by the kind of quiet that only nature can offer.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a family holiday, or a reunion with friends, the villa adapts to you. Spend mornings on the balcony with a cup of Coorg coffee, afternoons wandering the garden, and evenings around a crackling campfire under a sky full of stars.

  • A private swimming pool set against the estate's greenery

  • Perfect for couples, friends and large groups alike

Nearby Attractions

Explore Coorg From Your Doorstep

Mistora puts some of Coorg's most loved sights within easy reach.

Abbey Falls

A cascading waterfall framed by spice and coffee plantations, especially breathtaking after the monsoon.

Raja's Seat

A garden viewpoint famous for its sweeping sunset views over the valley.

Dubare Elephant Camp

Meet, feed and bathe with gentle elephants on the banks of the River Kaveri.

Golden Temple (Namdroling Monastery)

A vibrant Tibetan monastery with golden statues, prayer wheels and peaceful courtyards.

Mandalpatti

A rugged viewpoint offering panoramic views of mist-covered peaks — best reached by jeep.

Nisargadhama

A forested river island with bamboo groves, hanging bridges and a deer park.

Coffee Plantations

Guided walks through fragrant coffee and spice estates, right in Mistora's neighbourhood.
